NetSuite integration questions

Do you write SuiteScript?

Yes. We build Suitelets, RESTlets, scheduled scripts and user event scripts in SuiteScript 2.x, and connect outside systems through NetSuite's REST and SOAP web services.

Do we have to replace our other tools?

No. The point of an integration is to let each system do its job and stop people from retyping data between them. We connect what you already use.

How long does a NetSuite integration take?

Most automations go live within 2 to 4 weeks. Complex, multi-system integrations usually take 6 to 8 weeks. Your free assessment gives you a specific timeline.

What access do you need to our NetSuite account?

We work through a dedicated integration role with token-based authentication. You control the role's permissions and can revoke the tokens at any time.

What does it cost?

Every project is scoped to your process. The free assessment includes a preliminary cost range, and you get a fixed quote before any work starts.
