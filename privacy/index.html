Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Intellismart Group LLC (“Intellismart Group,” “we,” “us”) collects through intellismartgroup.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Information you send us. When you request a free assessment, we collect your company name, work email, the systems you use, the process you describe, whether you would like a call, and the page you came from. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Technical information. When you submit the assessment form, our server records your IP address so we can block spam and repeated automated submissions. Like most websites, the companies that host and deliver our pages may also log basic request details such as IP address, browser type and the page requested.

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers on this site, and the site does not set its own cookies.

How we use it

  • To review your request, prepare your assessment and reply to you.
  • To schedule and hold a call if you ask for one.
  • To keep the site and the form secure and free of spam.
  • To keep a record of our conversations if you become a client.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Who else handles it

We use a small number of service providers to run the site and respond to you. Each handles your information only as needed to provide its service:

  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages serves the website.
  • Form processing and storage: assessment requests are saved on our own server, hosted by Cloudways.
  • Email delivery: Postmark delivers the notification of your request to our inbox, and Microsoft 365 hosts that inbox.
  • Scheduling: if you book a call, Calendly collects your booking details under its own privacy policy, and the call takes place on Microsoft Teams.
  • Fonts and videos: pages load fonts from Google Fonts and video thumbnails from YouTube. A YouTube video loads only when you press play, and YouTube’s own privacy policy applies to that video.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

How long we keep it

We keep assessment requests and emails for as long as we need them to work with you, and longer only when we need records for legal, tax or accounting reasons. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Depending on where you live, including California, you may have additional rights under local privacy law. We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights. To make a request, email pohm@intellismartgroup.com.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) for the site and the form, and we limit access to your information to the people who need it to respond to you. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect what you share with us.

Children

This site is for businesses. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information: pohm@intellismartgroup.com.
