Accounting automation questions

Does this replace our accountants?

No. It removes the keying, matching and spreadsheet rebuilding. Your accountants review the results, handle the exceptions and approve what posts.

Will entries post to our books without review?

No. Every entry is shown on screen first, line by line, and only the people you choose can post it.

What happens with closed periods?

We never change a closed month. If something needs correcting, it is flagged for you and handled in the open period.

Which accounting systems do you work with?

Most of our accounting work is in NetSuite, connected to bank feeds, Ramp, Rippling and spreadsheets. The same approach works with other accounting systems that have an API.

How long does it take and what does it cost?

Most automations go live within 2 to 4 weeks; larger close projects usually take 6 to 8 weeks. The free assessment includes a preliminary cost range, and you get a fixed quote before any work starts.
