Where AI fits, and where it doesn't

Matching, calculations and anything that posts to your books run on fixed rules, so the answer is the same every time and you can audit it. We use AI where it is genuinely better than a rule: reading messy documents, drafting explanations and summaries, and finding records by plain-language search. A person approves anything that matters. That is how automation holds up after launch.

AI automation questions

Do we need our own AI or engineering team?

No. We build it, monitor it and fix it. Your team uses it the same way they use any other tool.

Is everything you build AI?

No. Matching, calculations and posting run on fixed rules so the results are the same every time. We use AI where it reads, drafts or explains, and a person approves anything that matters.

What happens to our data?

Your records stay in your own systems. An AI model only receives the data a task needs, through business API access, and we can keep sensitive steps entirely rule-based.

How long does a project take?

Most automations go live within 2 to 4 weeks. Complex integrations usually take 6 to 8 weeks. Your free assessment gives you a specific timeline.

What does it cost?

Every project is scoped to your process. The free assessment includes a preliminary cost range, and you get a fixed quote before any work starts.
